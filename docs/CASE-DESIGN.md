# Case Design — "Midnight Express: The Passenger Who Never Arrived"

**Status:** authoritative design document. All story data in `src/data/` must match this file.
**Constraints honoured:** no supernatural element, no secret twins, no hidden passages, no
culprit introduced at the ending, no violence. Every essential fact is reachable through
in-world clues before the final accusation.

---

## 1. Premise as the player experiences it

The night express *Vaskaya Meridian* runs six stops between Aldermere Junction and Vaskaya
Terminus. The player is **Ori Calder, 13**, an amateur detective riding to a school science
fair with a notebook and a stubborn habit of noticing things.

At 23:52 the train enters **Ferrow Tunnel**. Every light in the train dies for roughly forty
seconds. When they return, **Sleeper Compartment 4** will not open. Staff force it. Inside:
a grey herringbone coat, a wide-brim hat, a walking cane, an unmade bunk, and no passenger.

The compartment was booked to **"Mr. A. Ren."**

Four people were awake. All four give accounts that cannot all be true.

---

## 2. The true sequence of events

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 21:10 | **Nadia Vell** boards at Aldermere Junction. She carries two ticket stubs bought minutes apart at the same booking window: seat 12 in the passenger carriage (serial **4416**) and Sleeper Compartment 4 (serial **4417**, name "A. Ren"). |
| 21:20 | She stows a duffel in the baggage carriage and takes seat 12. Her manager **Teodor Kass** takes seat 3, watching her. |
| 22:05 | Nadia excuses herself "to sleep." In the sleeper vestibule she puts on the coat, hat and cane and enters Compartment 4 as "Mr. Ren." From this point "Ren" exists only as glimpses. |
| 22:30 | **Bram Oyelaran**, on his second consecutive shift, sits down in the staff nook of the baggage carriage "for one minute" and falls asleep. He stays asleep until 00:05. |
| 23:40 | **Ilse Perrot** mops the sleeper corridor and leaves the floor wet. She then returns to the dining carriage and, alone, counts the off-book cash from her private pastry sales and re-reads her acceptance letter from the Cormery Culinary School. |
| 23:47 | Kass buys cocoa at the dining counter (receipt timestamped 23:47) and immediately walks to the **baggage carriage** — the vestibule door is unlocked because Bram, asleep inside, never locked it. Kass intends to open the tour-company crate holding Nadia's grandmother's violin. |
| 23:50 | Kass breaks the wax seal on the crate. He re-presses it crookedly. A cufflink from his left sleeve drops behind the crate. |
| 23:52:14 | The train enters Ferrow Tunnel. The overhead line has a **neutral section** there; carriage lighting drops for 38–42 seconds on every run. Total darkness. |
| 23:52:20 | Nadia steps out of Compartment 4 wearing her own clothes, costume bundled under one arm. She pulls the fold-down bunk ladder into the sliding door's track and lets the door's stiff replacement spring draw it shut. The ladder wedges the door; from the corridor it now feels locked. |
| 23:52:30 | She pushes the costume into the **linen hamper** in the sleeper vestibule. |
| 23:52:40 | She walks into the adjacent dining carriage and stands at the counter in the dark, catching her breath. **Ilse sees a small silhouette at the counter** and assumes it is Kass returning. |
| 23:52:56 | Lights return. Nadia is already stepping through the far door. |
| 23:55 | Nadia is back in seat 12, reading. |
| 00:05 | Bram wakes, panics about the missing time, and begins a "patrol" he will later claim started at 23:45. |
| 00:10 | A passenger complains about a jammed door. Staff force Compartment 4. "Mr. Ren" is missing. |

**Why:** at the final stop Nadia is contracted to sign five years to Kass's touring agency.
She does not want to. Kass holds her travel papers and would physically block her from leaving
at any intermediate platform. But a **posted railway regulation** (Notice 12(b), framed in the
baggage carriage) states that a reported missing passenger forces an unscheduled hold at the
next station with **all passengers disembarked onto the platform for a roll call**. Nadia
invented a passenger so that the rule would put her, legally and in a crowd, onto the platform
at **Hollowmere** — the stop where her grandmother lives.

"A. Ren" is the wanderer from *The Wandering Ren*, the storybook her grandmother read to her.
A copy is in her bag.

**Nobody is hurt. Nothing is stolen by Nadia. The "crime" is a fiction she built to go home.**

---

## 3. The four answers the finale must produce

1. **Who was the missing passenger?** Nobody. "A. Ren" was Nadia Vell in a costume. The
   passenger who never arrived never boarded.
2. **How did the compartment appear locked?** The fold-down bunk ladder was wedged into the
   sliding door's floor track from inside as the door's stiff new spring pulled it closed.
   The door was never locked; it was jammed, and it opened four centimetres before stopping.
3. **How was the disappearance staged?** During the 38–42 second neutral-section blackout in
   Ferrow Tunnel, the costume was removed, the door jammed, the costume hidden in the sleeper
   vestibule's linen hamper, and Nadia walked one carriage forward to the dining counter before
   the lights returned.
4. **Why did the witnesses remember different details?** Nobody ever saw "Ren" properly —
   only a coat through frosted glass, a hat brim, a shape in a corridor — and each filled in
   an old man to match the name and the cane. On top of that, three of the four lied about
   their own position to hide an unrelated secret, and the fourth invented a vivid sighting
   to make Ren real.

## 4. Witnesses: claim vs. truth

### Bram Oyelaran — conductor, 54
- **Claims:** patrolled the sleeper corridor from 23:45, heard Compartment 4's lock click
  during the blackout.
- **Truth:** asleep in the baggage-carriage staff nook from 22:30 to 00:05. Second shift in
  a row.
- **Secret (unrelated):** sleeping on duty. He is nine weeks from a pension he cannot afford
  to lose; his daughter's tuition depends on it.
- **Breaks when:** shown the *Wet Corridor Notice* + *Footprint Survey*. A freshly mopped
  floor holds prints; only one small pair crossed it. Also his tea in the nook was still warm.
- **Emotion arc:** brisk → defensive → hesitant → relieved once told sleeping is not a crime.

### Ilse Perrot — dining attendant, 33
- **Claims:** the dining carriage was empty; she was in the galley with the door shut.
- **Truth:** she was at the counter the whole time and **saw a small figure in the dark**.
- **Secret (unrelated):** she sells her own pastries off the books to save for the Cormery
  Culinary School; the cash tin and acceptance letter are behind the counter.
- **Breaks when:** shown the *Cash Tin & Culinary Letter* — and treated kindly. She then gives
  the crucial revised testimony: a figure **about her own shoulder height**, smelling of pine
  rosin, stood at the counter during the blackout.
- **Emotion arc:** warm → avoidant → alarmed → relieved.

### Teodor Kass — tour manager, 46
- **Claims:** he was in the sleeper corridor knocking on Compartment 4, believing "Ren" was a
  rival scout.
- **Truth:** in the baggage carriage from 23:48, opening the crate with Nadia's grandmother's
  violin, intending to have it appraised as collateral against his debts.
- **Secret (unrelated to the disappearance, but ugly):** he is in debt; the signing advance is
  his lifeline.
- **Breaks when:** shown the *Broken Crate Seal* and his *Silver Cufflink* found behind it.
- **Emotion arc:** polished → dismissive → cornered → sullen honesty.

### Nadia Vell — violinist, 16
- **Claims:** never left seat 12; describes "Mr. Ren" in unusual detail — tall, stooped, grey
  coat, red scarf, silver-topped cane.
- **Truth:** she *was* Ren.
- **Secret:** the whole plan, and that she does not want to sign.
- **Breaks when:** the reconstruction is completed and the costume evidence is presented —
  hat band size 54, coat seams taken in with fresh thread, cane cut short, rosin in the collar,
  consecutive ticket serials.
- **Emotion arc:** friendly → over-helpful → hesitant → quiet, then honest.

## 6. The three major contradictions

**Contradiction I — "The Corridor That Kept No Footprints"**
Bram's statement (patrolled the sleeper corridor during the blackout) vs. `corridor`.
A floor mopped at 23:40 was still damp at 00:10 and held exactly one small pair of prints.
A conductor's boots (size 45, hobnailed) left none. → Bram was not there.
*Eliminates the alternative:* "the conductor let someone out of Compartment 4."

**Contradiction II — "The Cufflink Behind the Crate"**
Kass's statement (knocking at Compartment 4) vs. `cufflink` + `seal`.
He was two carriages away with his hand inside a crate. → Kass was not in the sleeper corridor.
*Eliminates the alternative:* "Kass abducted or hid the passenger."

**Contradiction III — "The Coat That Fit Nobody"**
The shared assumption (Ren was a tall, stooped old man) vs. `hat` (band 54) + `coat`
(seams taken in) + `cane` (shortened, tip unworn).
→ The clothes fit a person roughly 1.55 m tall who never leaned on the cane.
*Eliminates the alternative:* "a real elderly passenger left the compartment."

Each contradiction, once resolved, advances the stop counter and unlocks follow-up questions.

## 7. Mind Reconstruction — authored solution grid

Three moments × four suspects. Locations: Passenger, Dining, Sleeper, Baggage.

| | Before the tunnel (23:47) | During the blackout (23:52) | After the lights return (23:53) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bram | Baggage | Baggage | Baggage |
| Ilse | Dining | Dining | Dining |
| Kass | Dining | Baggage | Baggage |
| Nadia | Sleeper | Dining | Passenger |

Conflict rules, each gated behind the evidence that proves it (a rule stays silent if the
player has not discovered its evidence — the mode never leaks unknown facts):

- Bram in Sleeper at any moment → `corridor` ("no boot prints on the wet floor").
- Kass in Sleeper at blackout/after → `cufflink` ("his cufflink was behind the crate").
- Kass in Baggage before the tunnel → `receipt` ("he was buying cocoa at 23:47").
- Ilse anywhere but Dining → `tin` ("her tin and letter were open behind the counter").
- Nadia in Passenger during the blackout → Ilse's revised testimony + `rosin`.
- Nadia not in Sleeper before the tunnel → `ticket_nadia` + `ticket_ren`.
- Nadia not in Passenger after the lights → three passengers saw her reading at 23:55.
- Anyone moving Passenger↔Baggage between two adjacent moments → travel-time conflict
  (four carriage lengths in 40 seconds in total darkness), gated behind `neutral_section`.
- Two suspects assigned to Compartment 4's carriage during the blackout when only one small
  set of prints exists → `corridor`.

The mode is always labelled **THEORY — not a recording** on screen.

## 9. Epilogue — everyone's secret

- **Nadia** gets off at Hollowmere. Ori does not report the ticket; the railway files "A. Ren"
  as a no-show, which is exactly what it was. She writes to the agency herself.
- **Kass** is reported to the railway for opening the crate. The violin goes home with Nadia.
- **Bram** admits the double shift to the depot. The rostering, not the man, gets disciplined.
- **Ilse** hands in her notice and takes the Cormery place. Ori buys the last pastry.
- **Ori** writes it up as *Case 14: The Passenger Who Never Arrived* and notes in the margin:
  *"Not every disappearance is a crime. Some are just someone going home."*
